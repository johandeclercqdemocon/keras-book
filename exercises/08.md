# Exercises — Chapter 8

Chapter: [Overfitting and regularization](../chapters/08-overfitting-and-regularization.md) · module: [`examples/ch08_overfitting_and_regularization.py`](../examples/ch08_overfitting_and_regularization.py) · solutions: [08](solutions/08.md)

**1. [measure]** Repeat the regularizer table with three different seeds for the baseline and for dropout 0.5. Report the test accuracy for each seed and the spread of each setting. Expected: the spread within a setting is 0.004, and the sign of the difference between the settings is not the same as in the single-seed table of the chapter.

**2. [break]** Train the dropout 0.5 model and compare `model(x_test, training=True)` with `model(x_test, training=False)` on the same input. Show that the two outputs differ, and show that two calls with `training=False` are equal. Say what this means if you forget to pass `training=False` when you serve the model.

**3. [judge]** The learning curve is flat from 800 to 1,347 images. A manager asks the team to stop collecting data. What does a good answer weigh?
