# Exercises — Chapter 12

Chapter: [Attention and transformers](../chapters/12-attention-and-transformers.md) · module: [`examples/ch12_attention_and_transformers.py`](../examples/ch12_attention_and_transformers.py) · solutions: [12](solutions/12.md)

**1. [measure]** Count the weights of `MultiHeadAttention(num_heads=4, key_dim=16)` on an input of width 64. Do it by hand from the structure in the chapter, then with `count_params()`. Expected: 3 × (64 × 4 × 16 + 4 × 16) + (4 × 16 × 64 + 64) = 16,640.

**2. [break]** Build the `TransformerBlock` of the chapter with a global average pool, with no position embedding. Give it a batch and the same batch with the tokens of every sequence in a different order. Show that the outputs are equal. Then add `AddPositions` and show that they differ. Expected: the first difference is below 1e-5, and the second is not.

**3. [judge]** A team must classify call logs of 4,000 tokens. A colleague says that attention costs `T²`, so a GRU is the only choice. What does a good answer weigh?
