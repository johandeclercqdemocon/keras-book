**1. [measure]** A network has `Conv2D(8, 3, padding="valid")`, `MaxPooling2D(2)`, `Conv2D(16, 3, padding="valid")` and `GlobalMaxPooling2D()` on an input of 8 × 8 × 1. Report the shape after each layer and the total number of convolution weights. Expected: shapes (6, 6, 8), (3, 3, 8), (1, 1, 16), (16,), and 80 + 1,168 = 1,248 weights.

**2. [break]** Take the `cnn global` model of the chapter and shift the test images by two pixels to the right. Report the accuracy. Then say why the result is different from the one-pixel result. Expected: the accuracy falls below 0.99.

**3. [judge]** A colleague says "use a CNN for everything with a grid, because convolution is translation invariant". What does a good answer weigh?
