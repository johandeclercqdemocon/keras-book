# Exercises — Chapter 14

Chapter: [Saving, loading and export](../chapters/14-saving-loading-export.md) · module: [`examples/ch14_saving_loading_export.py`](../examples/ch14_saving_loading_export.py) · solutions: [14](solutions/14.md)

**1. [measure]** Train a model of three `Dense` layers with Adam and save it. Count the arrays in the `optimizer` group of the weights file. Predict the number first. Expected: 2 + 2 × 6 = 14, because there are 6 weight arrays, and Adam keeps two numbers for each plus the step count and the learning rate.

**2. [break]** Save the weights of a model with two `Dense(4)` layers and a `relu` after the first. Load them into a model with the same shapes and the `relu` after the second. Report whether the load raises an error and whether the predictions are equal. Expected: no error, and the predictions differ.

**3. [judge]** The serving team asks for "the model file". Do you give them the `.keras` file or the exported SavedModel? What does a good answer weigh?
