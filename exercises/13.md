# Exercises — Chapter 13

Chapter: [Transfer learning](../chapters/13-transfer-learning.md) · module: [`examples/ch13_transfer_learning.py`](../examples/ch13_transfer_learning.py) · solutions: [13](solutions/13.md)

**1. [measure]** Build the frozen model of the chapter and count its trainable weights and all of its weights. Expected: 330 trainable weights of 5,130.

**2. [break]** Build a frozen model, compile it, train one epoch, then set `trainable = True` on the base **without** compiling again, and train one more epoch. Report whether the base weights changed. Then repeat with a new `compile`. Expected: `False`, then `True`.

**3. [judge]** A team has a model that a vendor trained on 10 million images from another domain. They have 200 labeled images of their own. Should they freeze the vendor model, fine-tune it, or train from scratch? What does a good answer weigh?
