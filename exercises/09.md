# Exercises — Chapter 9

Chapter: [Custom losses, metrics and training steps](../chapters/09-custom-training.md) · module: [`examples/ch09_custom_training.py`](../examples/ch09_custom_training.py) · solutions: [09](solutions/09.md)

**1. [measure]** Train the model of the metric section for 3 epochs. Then call `evaluate` on the test images with `batch_size=1` and with `batch_size=450`. Report `balanced_accuracy` for both. Expected: the two values are equal to 5 digits, because the metric keeps counts and not a mean of batches.

**2. [break]** Change `reset_state` of `BalancedAccuracy` so that it does nothing. Train for 3 epochs with validation data. Compare the `val_balanced_accuracy` of the third epoch with the correct value from scikit-learn. Expected: the broken value is not equal to the correct one, and the metric is a mean over all epochs.

**3. [judge]** A teammate wants label smoothing on a TensorFlow-only project and plans to write a custom `train_step` with `tf.GradientTape` to get it. What does a good answer weigh?
