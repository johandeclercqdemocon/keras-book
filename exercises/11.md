# Exercises — Chapter 11

Chapter: [Sequences](../chapters/11-sequences.md) · module: [`examples/ch11_sequences.py`](../examples/ch11_sequences.py) · solutions: [11](solutions/11.md)

**1. [measure]** Compute by hand the number of weights of the `gru` model: an `Embedding(10, 16)`, a `GRU(32)` and a `Dense(1)`. Then check the number with `count_params()`. Use this fact: a GRU with input width `d` and `u` units has `3 × (d × u + u × u + 2 × u)` weights. Expected: 160 + 4,800 + 33 = 4,993.

**2. [break]** The chapter offers one candidate for the shape of the `conv` curve: tokens at the edge of the sequence appear in fewer windows. Test it. Train the `conv` model, then score it only on test pairs with a distance of 3 or more. Split the pairs into those where both tokens are at positions 4 to 11, and those where at least one token is at position 0, 1, 14 or 15. Report both accuracies. Expected: the interior pairs score near 0.5, and the edge pairs score near 1.0.

**3. [judge]** A team classifies call transcripts into 12 topics. A bag-of-words model scores 0.88 and trains in a minute. A recurrent model scores 0.89 and trains in an hour. Which one does the team ship? What does a good answer weigh?
