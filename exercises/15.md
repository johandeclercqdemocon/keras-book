**1. [measure]** Run the determinism probe 3 times with `TF_DETERMINISTIC_OPS=1` and compare the final losses. Report the largest difference. Expected: below 1e-6. Do not expect equal weights.

**2. [break]** Turn determinism on, build a small model without calling `set_random_seed`, and call `fit` for one epoch. Report the first words of the error. Then add the seed and show that the error is gone. Expected: the message starts with "Random ops require a seed".

**3. [judge]** A colleague shows that mixed precision made training 1.8 times faster on a GPU, and wants it for every project. What does a good answer weigh?
